# IVNM Voice

Autonomous English educational TTS. CPU inference, no paid API. Only synthetic public test scripts. Never submit student data or private materials to public workflows.

## Voice casting

Pocket Marius is rejected by listening review. The former Kitten second voice Bruno is retired from benchmark rotation as a precaution, not independently judged. Pocket Charles and Paul, Kitten Jasper, and Chatterbox Charles VCTK reference are experimental candidates, not approved voices. Chatterbox previously used only one speaker; the next benchmark tests a real two-speaker dialogue. VCTK reference: CSTR VCTK Corpus v0.92, Yamagishi, Veaux and MacDonald, University of Edinburgh, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645, CC BY 4.0; enhanced source via https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices. No rejected voice may be used in current render plans; keep previous samples for comparison.

## Voice bank v1
12 mandatory categories (British/Canadian × child/teen/adult × male/female), two desired approved variants per category. See voice-bank.json. All initial slots are pending real audition; this is a requirement registry, not a claim of 24 available voices. No real minor reference may be used without appropriate permission and rights; prefer synthetic age-appropriate voices.
