# IVNM synthetic casting, round 1

Every British/Canadian × child/teen/adult × male/female category receives two stochastic synthetic reference candidates, each tested with a second emotional utterance conditioned on the reference. No real-person reference audio is used. These are perceived voice ages, not actual people. Output is not approved automatically. Check accent, perceived age, timbre, prosody, stability and intelligibility by listening. Canadian English requires a dedicated accent review; prompts alone do not verify it.

Model: OpenBMB VoxCPM2 (Apache-2.0), source https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM, pinned code revision 300e4260b509066151d623dc9c0aa922cf82232b. CPU inference is experimental; failure or timeouts are possible. Artifacts contain synthetic WAV masters, MP3 previews and provenance/seed reports. Do not upload confidential or student scripts. The central voice bank remains unchanged until audition approval.

## Review result
Round 1 completed, but VoxCPM2 was rejected by the user on listening quality. Its automated workflow is retired; previous artifacts remain historical only. Future casting should focus on Pocket, KittenTTS 2 and Chatterbox. None of the generated VoxCPM2 voices are eligible for the approved bank.
